# Charter inquiry template

Use known facts; mark undecided items.

**Trip:** one way / round trip / multi-leg.

| Leg | From | To | Local date/time or arrival deadline | Passengers |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | City + selected airport/code | City + selected airport/code | Year and time zone | Supplied party size |

**Flexibility:** date range, departure window, alternate airports and ground-transfer tolerance.

**Party needs:** baggage/equipment; pets and sizes; accessibility; cabin requests.

**Budget:** whole-aircraft/trip amount and currency, or not specified.

**Please confirm:** aircraft and operating carrier; nonstop feasibility; complete price/exclusions; exact schedule; pet/baggage approval; cancellation/refund/replacement terms; quote expiry.

**Contact:** only if supplied for this recipient and the traveler wants the inquiry sent.

**Status:** draft / estimate / submitted inquiry. A request is not a reservation.
